**Dongjie Wang** is a Professor in the School of Information Science and Technology at [Northeast Normal University](https://www.nenu.edu.cn/) (东北师范大学), and a recipient of the NSFC Excellent Young Scientists Fund Program (Overseas). Previously, he was an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at the [University of Kansas](https://ku.edu/) (2024–2026).

His research interests lie in **LLM agents**, **reinforcement learning**, **data-centric AI**, **causal graph learning**, **spatial-temporal data mining**, and **automated urban planning**.

He has published over 70 papers in top-tier conferences and journals, including *NeurIPS*, *KDD*, *AAAI*, *WWW*, *TKDE*, and *KAIS*. His work has received multiple recognitions, including three Best Paper Runner-up/Finalist Awards at [SIGSPATIAL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3397536.3422268) and [ICDM](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9679029), a Spotlight Paper at [NeurIPS 2023](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/8797d13e5998acfab387d4bf0a5b9b00-Abstract-Conference.html), and a Best Paper Candidate Award at ACM CIKM 2025.

His research on automated urban planning has been featured by [Synced AI](https://medium.com/syncedreview/can-ai-reimagine-city-configuration-and-automate-urban-planning-121666e509da) and [UCF Today](https://www.ucf.edu/news/where-artificial-intelligence-meets-urban-planning/), highlighting the broader societal impact of his work.

He actively serves the research community as a program committee member or reviewer for *NeurIPS*, *ICML*, *KDD*, *CVPR*, *AAAI*, *WWW*, *TPAMI*, *TKDD*, and *Nature Cities*, and as an editor for *npj Urban Sustainability*.
